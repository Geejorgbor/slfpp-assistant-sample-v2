Society For Less Fortunate People Project serves people who need structured support — including small livelihood loans. 1. Built around how SLFPP actually works

People walk into the program from communities across Monrovia and beyond. Field officers verify them. Loans are opened for livelihoods such as market stalls, catering, agriculture inputs, and trades. Installments fall on set dates. Staff need to know — without hunting — who registered this week, what each person still owes, and who should pay today.

“Mary Kollie should pay today” should appear on a screen before anyone has to remember it. A new intake should never leave without a record. A loan’s full payment history should open in seconds.

2. Three modules — complete enough for daily operations

Incoming registration

Capture full name, phone, community, ID, reason for coming in, livelihood, next of kin, preferred WhatsApp/SMS channel, field officer, referral source, and intake status (new → pending verification → eligible for loan screening). Recent intakes stay searchable so supervisors can review the week’s walk-ins.

Loan track record

Per member: every loan opened, purpose, principal in LRD, balance remaining, percent paid, open date, status (active/closed), and a dated timeline of disbursements and installment payments — including prior closed loans for the same person.

Payment-due reminders

Morning list of who should pay today (amount + loan ID + channel), overdue members who missed yesterday, and upcoming dues later this week. Staff queue a WhatsApp or SMS reminder, then mark paid when cash or mobile money lands.

4. How staff use reminders each morning

  • Open Reminders — see the day’s due list (example: Mary Kollie, LRD 25,000, LN-1042, WhatsApp).
  • Act on overdue first — members who missed yesterday stay highlighted until cleared.
  • Queue outreach — send reminder on the member’s preferred channel.
  • Mark paid — when cash, Orange Money, Lonestar MTN, or other mobile money arrives, update the record.
  • Glance at upcoming — plan mid-week outreach before installment dates hit.
