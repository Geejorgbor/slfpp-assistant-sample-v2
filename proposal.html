02 · Context

03 · System structure

A complete loan management system — module by module

Branches

A Main Branch plus satellite offices (e.g. Red Light, Sinkor & West Point). Borrowers, loans, staff and collections belong to a branch; switch branch to scope the whole workspace. Add branches as SLFPP grows.

Loan products

Category, amount range, duration in weeks or months, simple interest (including 0% welfare loans), repayment cycle — weekly, monthly or custom every N days — optional fees, late penalties and required deposit.

Registration & borrowers

Walk-in intake through verification to eligibility, then a full borrower profile: date of birth, gender, ID and tax number, employment, business, monthly income, next of kin, documents checklist and uploads.

Loans

Borrower + product, item / principal value, initial deposit, period — the installment is calculated automatically, with interest and fees on the actual principal financed. Collateral note and photo, approver notes.

Approval & schedule

Status moves Processing → Approved → Active → Closed. Approval generates the full payment schedule — every due date with principal, interest and fee split — stored on the loan.

Due payments

Every unpaid installment across all schedules, filtered: All · Overdue · Due today · Next 3 days · This week · This month — with penalties, branch, channel and one-click collect.

Flexible repayments

Select one or several unpaid installments, pay ahead, choose cash or mobile money, record receipt and reference, collector and note. Paid weeks lock; loan and portfolio percentages update instantly. Printable receipts.

Reminders & track record

Morning reminder queue built from live schedules on each borrower’s WhatsApp / SMS channel; full loan history per borrower with timeline from application to final payment.

Dashboard & reports

Live portfolio numbers, repaid % by branch, portfolio at risk, collections by receipt, due lists and intakes — print for the board or export CSV.

04 · Loan lifecycle

From application to final payment — every step recorded

  1. Create — a field officer picks the borrower and product, enters item value (e.g. a sewing machine at LRD 140,000) and initial deposit (LRD 20,000). The system shows principal financed, interest, fees, total repayable and the installment. Status: Processing.
  2. Review & approve — Admin or Branch Manager checks documents, income vs installment and other loans, then approves. The full schedule is generated automatically.
  3. Disburse — cash, mobile money, bank or direct supplier payment for items. The loan becomes Active and the first installment appears in Due Payments and Reminders.
  4. Collect — staff record repayments against one or many installments, including paying ahead, with receipt, reference, collector and note. Late penalties apply per product rules.
  5. Close — when the last installment is paid the loan closes automatically and stays in the borrower’s track record.
05 · Controls & reporting

Role-based access, branch scoping and board-ready reports

Staff login & roles

Admin sees all branches, products, approvals, reports and settings. Branch Manager (a custom role) approves and disburses for one branch. Field Officers register borrowers, prepare loans and collect. More custom roles (e.g. Cashier) can be created.

Built-in controls

Approval required before disbursement, locked paid installments, deposit and amount-range checks per product, and every action tied to a named staff account and branch.

Borrower & loan records

One click opens the full borrower profile or loan statement — terms, lifecycle, collateral, schedule, receipts — printable for files or meetings.

Printable reports & CSV

Portfolio by branch and product, portfolio at risk, collections by receipt, due list and new intakes — print for the monthly board meeting or export CSV for partners.

07 · Daily routine

How staff run collections each morning

  1. Open Due Payments — start with Overdue (e.g. Korto Mulbah, LN-1095, 6 days late with LRD 500 penalty), then Due today.
  2. Queue reminders — WhatsApp or SMS on each borrower’s preferred channel.
  3. Record repayments — select installments (or pay ahead), method, mobile money reference, receipt.
  4. Check this week — plan the 3-day and weekly lists so market-day collections are not rushed.
  5. Supervisor review — dashboard shows collected today, repaid % by branch and loans waiting for approval.
