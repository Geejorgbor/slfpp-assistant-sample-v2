3. How reminders work each morning

  • Due today — staff see who should pay today with LRD amount and loan number.
  • Overdue — missed installments stay visible until cleared.
  • Upcoming this week — plan outreach before the due date.
  • Queue WhatsApp / SMS — send reminder, then mark paid when money lands.
