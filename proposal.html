3 How staff use reminders each morning

  • Due today list — open Reminders and see names like “Mary Kollie should pay today” with LRD amount and loan number.
  • Overdue flags — missed installments surface in red before they stack into larger arrears.
  • Upcoming this week — plan outreach before the due date so collections stay on track.
  • Queue WhatsApp / SMS — mark reminder sent, then mark paid when cash or mobile money lands.
  • Dashboard mirror — the same due-today people appear on the Overview so supervisors see the day at a glance.
