02 · Context

Built around how SLFPP actually works

People walk into the program from communities across Monrovia and beyond. Field officers verify them. Loans support livelihoods such as market stalls, catering, agriculture inputs and trades. Installments fall on set dates. Staff need one credible screen — not scattered paper.

“Mary Kollie should pay today” should appear before anyone has to remember it. A new intake should never leave without a record. A loan’s full payment history should open in seconds.

03 · Core modules

Three modules — complete enough for daily operations

Incoming registration

Full name, phone, community, ID, reason for coming in, livelihood, next of kin, preferred WhatsApp/SMS channel, field officer, referral source, and intake status through loan screening.

Loan track record

Per member: every loan, purpose, principal and balance in LRD, percent paid, open date, status, and a dated timeline of disbursements and installments — including closed loans.

Payment-due reminders

Due today, overdue, and upcoming this week — with amount, loan ID, and channel. Queue WhatsApp or SMS, then mark paid when cash or mobile money lands.

04 · Controls & reporting

Built-in staff controls and board-ready reporting

Staff login & roles

Each staff member signs in. Admin sees reports, settings and loan disbursement; Field Officers focus on registration, member profiles and payment reminders.

Member profiles

One click opens the full person: identity, registration details, every loan, every payment, next due, and preferred WhatsApp/SMS reminder channel.

Disburse loan + schedule

Choose member, principal in LRD, term in months and purpose — the system builds the installment schedule and feeds reminders automatically.

Printable reports & CSV

Collection summary by community, due list and new intakes — print for the monthly meeting or export CSV for Excel and partners.

06 · Daily routine

How staff use reminders each morning

  1. Open Reminders — see the day’s due list (example: Mary Kollie, LRD 25,000, LN-1042, WhatsApp).
  2. Act on overdue first — members who missed yesterday stay visible until cleared.
  3. Queue outreach — send reminder on the member’s preferred channel.
  4. Mark paid — when cash or mobile money arrives, update the record.
  5. Check upcoming — plan mid-week outreach before installment dates hit.
