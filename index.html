Payment-due reminders

Built from the live repayment schedules: who is overdue, who should pay today, and who is due later this week — queue a WhatsApp / SMS reminder or mark paid.

Morning collection routine: Open Reminders → clear overdue first → work the due-today list → queue reminders on each borrower’s preferred channel → mark paid when cash or mobile money arrives (it posts a receipted repayment) → check upcoming so mid-week dues are planned, not rushed.
