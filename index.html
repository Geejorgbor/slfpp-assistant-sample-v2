Incoming Registration

Register people coming into the program with identity, contact, community, livelihood, and intake status.

How SLFPP intake works in this assistant: A person walks in from a community (e.g. Red Light, Paynesville, Sinkor). Staff capture identity, phone, livelihood, next of kin, and preferred WhatsApp/SMS channel. Status moves from new intake → pending verification → eligible for loan screening. That same channel is used later when an installment is due.

Loan Track Record

Open any member to see principal, balance remaining, percent paid, and a full payments timeline.

What staff see per member: Every livelihood loan opened (market stall, catering, agri inputs, trades), principal and balance in LRD, percent paid, purpose, open date, and a full timeline of disbursements and installments — including closed loans for the same person. Payment-due reminders

See who should pay today, who is overdue, and who is due later this week — then queue a reminder or mark paid.

Morning collection routine: Open Reminders → clear overdue first → work the due-today list (name, LRD amount, loan ID, WhatsApp or SMS) → queue reminder → mark paid when cash or mobile money arrives → check upcoming this week so mid-week dues are planned, not rushed.

Disburse new loan

Create a livelihood loan for a member and generate the repayment schedule before money leaves the office.

Admin approval step: Choose the member, enter principal in LRD, term in months and purpose. The assistant builds the monthly installment schedule instantly. Confirming adds the loan to the member’s track record and profile, and the first installment goes into reminders.
